Mission

Our mission

To connect and empower stakeholders to achieve sustainable community and economic development through the transformation of food systems — turning grassroots needs into resourced, lasting outcomes.

Why Bottom Up

The gap between grassroots needs and institutional resources rarely closes on its own.

We serve as a trusted convener and connector — aligning vision, finance and innovation to co-create resilient and inclusive food systems.

We bridge grassroots realities and institutional resources so that the solutions that reach communities are practical, scalable and genuinely community-driven.

What we do

Four ways we hold the bridge steady

Convening & partnerships

Multi-stakeholder dialogues and coalition building that bring communities, government and the private sector to the same table.

Finance & brokerage

Connecting communities with funders, investors and public-private partnerships to unlock capital for food systems.

Infrastructure & innovation

Deploying practical, climate-smart systems that hold up under real conditions on the ground.

Advisory & capacity building

Governance, project management and knowledge sharing that strengthen local ownership over time.

Impact

What no single actor could accomplish alone.

By serving as a bridge, Bottom Up strengthens food security, creates green jobs, and accelerates progress towards the Sustainable Development Goals.

Food security

Resilient, climate-smart food systems built with the communities who depend on them.

Green jobs

Economic opportunity that grows alongside sustainable infrastructure and innovation.

The SDGs

Coordinated progress that only happens when finance, policy and community align.
